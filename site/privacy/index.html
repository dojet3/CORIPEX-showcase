Privacy

Updated: October 16, 2026

This CORIPEX Showcase is a static public website. The interactive demo runs locally in the visitor's browser and uses predefined content. It does not offer accounts, uploads, or live external AI requests.

Browser behavior

This version includes no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, contact forms or third-party media embeds. External source links are opened only when you select them. A hosting provider may process routine network request data when the site is deployed.

Contact by email

If you email info@coripex.com, we receive the address you use and the information you choose to send. Inbound mail is routed through Cloudflare Email Routing to a designated mailbox. We use messages to respond to inquiries and manage the correspondence. Please do not email passwords, API keys, or private research documents. For privacy questions about correspondence, contact info@coripex.com.

Protected application

The public Showcase is separate from the protected CORIPEX application. If application access becomes available, that service will have its own applicable account and privacy information.
