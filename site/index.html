The intelligence engine

Custom-Engineered RAG Pipeline

Beyond retrieval. Intelligence by design.

At the heart of CORIPEX is a purpose-built Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline, engineered for security, source traceability, and controlled research workflows.

Instead of depending on a generic end-to-end RAG framework, CORIPEX coordinates dedicated components for hybrid retrieval, context engineering, governed generation, and backend-controlled citations. Explore the stages below.

PIPELINE ARCHITECTURESelect a stage to learn more ↘
01Source & Access ScopeAuthorized workspaces, documents and provenance+

Research begins inside an authorized Workspace. Document identity, version history, and PostgreSQL access rules are preserved before evidence is used by downstream systems.

↓
02ALexical RetrievalPostgreSQL full-text search+

Term-based retrieval finds passages that match explicit research language while respecting Workspace scope and source eligibility.

02BSemantic RetrievalVector search with Qdrant+

Semantic candidates are retrieved from compatible vector indexes. Retrieved records must be rechecked against authoritative authorization and source state.

↓
03Fusion & Context EngineeringRank fusion, deduplication, evidence selection and token budgets+

Reciprocal Rank Fusion combines search channels. A dedicated ContextBuilder selects bounded, diverse, source-linked evidence and limits what may be transmitted to an AI provider.

↓
04Governed GenerationPolicy checks, redaction and structured output+

Model access passes through explicit provider policies, consent, data classification and minimization. Structured generation is coordinated by the application, not by the retrieved documents.

↓
05Source-Linked Research AnswersBackend-controlled citation identity and evidence checks+

Answer records preserve versioned citations and supporting source references. When the available evidence is insufficient, the workflow is designed to communicate that limitation rather than invent supporting facts.

Architecture & engineering

Serious architecture. Practical by design.

CORIPEX is built as a security-first modular monolith: clear domain boundaries, a focused deployment model, and independent runtime responsibilities where isolation matters.

PostgreSQL anchors authoritative data and workspace permissions. Redis coordinates background work, while Qdrant supports vector retrieval as a rebuildable index. A governed AI layer separates retrieval, context construction, generation, and source verification.

Our engineering principles

Built on principles. Engineered for trust.

Research intelligence deserves more than an impressive answer. It needs secure foundations, traceable evidence, resilient systems, and room to evolve.

01

Security First

Protection is part of the architecture, from private workspaces and database-level isolation to secure document intake and controlled AI access.

02

Evidence Before Answers

Research starts with sources. Retrieval, provenance, and citations are designed into the workflow rather than added as an afterthought.

03

Knowledge Outlives Models

Documents, metadata, and source history remain authoritative; indexes and AI components can evolve around them.

04

Small Footprint. Big Architecture.

A focused modular core today, with explicit boundaries for more sophisticated capabilities tomorrow.

Designed to be tested, reviewed, and improved. Versioned data, automated checks, independent reviews, and failure-aware workflows are part of how CORIPEX is engineered.
